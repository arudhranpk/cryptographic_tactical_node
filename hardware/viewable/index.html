[AUDIT PASS] Sheet 2: Power Management (2S Li-ion + TPS563201 Buck + USB Direct Bypass)
Verified Clean

Architecture Topology: Two 18650 Li-ion cells in series (Keystone 1042 holders BT1, BT2; 6.8V to 8.4V pack) feed an active P-MOSFET reverse polarity protection circuit (FDN304PZ Q1 with 5.6V Zener clamp D2), through a 1.85A hold / 3.7A trip PPTC fuse (F1, Bel Fuse 0ZCF0185FF2C, SMD 2920), into an authentic 3A SPDT slide switch (SW1: SS-12D10L7-XKB). The switched +VCOM bus drives a TI TPS563201 synchronous step-down converter delivering 5.0V at 92% efficiency to power the LoRA transmitter and GPS, and dual TLV74333 LDOs for clean 3.3V analog/digital isolation.

Key Audit Findings:
• Switch Wiring Parity: Pin 2 connects to fused battery, Pin 3 connects to +VCOM (ON state), Pin 1 is unconnected (NC).
• USB Bypass Diode: D3 (SS24B 40V 2A Schottky) delivers 5V from USB-C directly to the +5V system rail with SW1 OFF.
• Dropout Headroom: TPS563201 enters 92% maximum duty cycle dropout at $V_{in} \le 5.43 ext{ V}$. At 6.8V battery cutoff, input headroom is $1.37 ext{ V}$, providing continuous 5.00V regulation without hiccup.
[AUDIT PASS] Sheet 3: STM32F411CEUx Microcontroller Subsystem
Verified Clean

Clocks & Decoupling: High Speed External (HSE) 25.000 MHz crystal Y1 with load caps C19 (33pF) / C25 (22pF); Low Speed External (LSE) 32.768 kHz RTC crystal Y2 with C28 (18pF) / C33 (11pF). VDD pins 24, 36, 48 decoupled by local 100nF ceramic caps plus 4.7µF bulk capacitor C27. Core voltage regulator pin VCAP1 (Pin 22) stabilized by dedicated 4.7µF ceramic cap C29. Analog domain VDDA (Pin 9) isolated via BLM18AG121 ferrite bead FB1 and decoupled by C30 (100nF).

[AUDIT PASS] Sheet 4: u-blox NEO-M8N Concurrent GNSS Receiver
Verified Clean

Interface: Communicates with STM32 via USART2 (PA2 TX, PA3 RX). Powered from +5V rail with local 100nF decoupling. Onboard active antenna bias network and backup battery support for warm-start TTFF < 2 seconds.

[AUDIT PASS] Sheet 5: Ebyte E32-900T20D Long-Range LoRA Transceiver
Verified Clean

Power & RF Grounding: Module VCC powered directly from +5V rail with parallel 47µF bulk electrolytic/ceramic capacitors (C38, C39) to buffer +20 dBm (120 mA) RF transmit bursts. Mode control lines M0 (PB15) and M1 (PB14) pulled down by R30 and R29. AUX pin (PB5) routed to STM32 for TX/RX completion monitoring. Unrouted pins 8, 9, 10 marked NC per D-18 to eliminate ground loop antennas.

[AUDIT PASS] Sheet 6: NXP SE050C2HQ1 Hardware Secure Element (EAL 6+)
Verified Clean

Cryptographic Root-of-Trust: Powered from isolated +CRYPT_3V3 LDO rail. I2C1 bus (PB8 SCL, PB9 SDA) configured for Fast-mode (400 kHz) with pull-up resistors R24 (4.7kΩ) and R25 (2.2kΩ) tied to +STM_3V3 per D-17. Autonomous I2C wake-up on start condition.

[AUDIT PASS] Sheet 7: Physical Enclosure Anti-Tamper Intrusion Detection
Verified Clean

Autonomous Zeroization: Miniature SMD enclosure switch S4 connects to STM32 Pin 2 (PC13 / RTC_TAMP1). S4 is held depressed (closed to GND) by the enclosure lid. If opened, R31 (33kΩ) pulls PC13 HIGH, triggering autonomous hardware wiping of all 20 RTC backup registers (80 bytes) in 4 RTCCLK cycles, even under deep sleep or battery backup. C42 (100nF) suppresses RF false triggers from the LoRA antenna.
