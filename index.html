STM32F411CE Pinout & 5V Tolerance Visual Guide

UFQFPN-48 Physical Package

🚫 Only 1 GPIO is NOT 5V Tolerant

PA0 (Pin 10) is the only GPIO with a TC structure. Strictly a 3.3V input (Max 3.6V). 5V will permanently damage PA0!

✅ 35 GPIOs ARE 5V Tolerant

All other GPIOs (PA1–PA15, PB0–PB15, PC13–PC15, PH0–PH1) have FT structure and safely accept 5V in standard digital mode.

⚠️ ADC & Oscillator Warning

Pins PA1–PA7 & PB0–PB1 lose 5V tolerance when used as ADC inputs. PH0/PH1 and PC14/PC15 lose it when crystals are enabled.

KiCad 10.0.6 Electrical Rules Check (ERC) & Design Audit

12 Findings (2 Errors, 10 Warnings)
[ERC ERROR] Sheet /Power/: Power Output Pin Collision on +STM_3V3
Critical KiCad Error

Locations: #FLG05 Pin 1 [Power output] @ (95.25mm, 190.50mm) ↔ U2 Pin 5 [OUT, Power output] @ (255.27mm, 30.48mm).

Root Cause: KiCad flags an error when two pins of type Power output are connected. TLV74333 LDO pin 5 is defined as type Power output. Placing PWR_FLAG (#FLG05) directly on +STM_3V3 causes KiCad to detect duplicate power drivers.

Remediation: Remove PWR_FLAG #FLG05 from net +STM_3V3. The TLV74333 already serves as the recognized power source for this rail.
[ERC ERROR] Sheet /Power/: Power Output Pin Collision on +CRYPT_3V3
Critical KiCad Error

Locations: #FLG07 Pin 1 [Power output] @ (107.95mm, 190.50mm) ↔ U3 Pin 5 [OUT, Power output] @ (255.27mm, 72.39mm).

Root Cause: Identical to U2: TLV74333 LDO U3 pin 5 is defined as Power output. Connecting PWR_FLAG #FLG07 on +CRYPT_3V3 causes KiCad to detect two simultaneous power drivers.

Remediation: Remove PWR_FLAG #FLG07 from net +CRYPT_3V3.
[ERC WARNING] Sheet /Power/: Net Name Conflict (CE, PROG2, SEL on VUSB Bus)
Netlist Ambiguity

Locations: Global labels CE @ (177.8mm, 143.51mm) and PROG2 @ (177.8mm, 133.35mm) wired together on vertical bus line connected to VUSB (#PWR079).

Root Cause: MCP73871 control inputs SEL, PROG2, and CE are all pulled high to VUSB. Wiring multiple global labels directly to the same conductor shorts their net names together and produces netlist confusion.

Remediation: Remove global labels CE and PROG2 from the bus wire, or tie each pin to +VBUS directly without conflicting global labels.
[FIRMWARE / IOC BUG] STM32CubeMX: PB14 (LORA_M1) Configured as GPIO_Input
Firmware Lockout

File: software/stm32cube/crypt_node.ioc (Line 79: PB14.Signal=GPIO_Input).

Root Cause: In Ebyte E32-900T20D, both M0 and M1 are control inputs. To switch operating modes (Sleep, Power-saving, Normal), the STM32 must drive both pins. With PB14 configured as GPIO_Input, the MCU pin remains high-impedance (pulled Low by R29 10k), preventing the node from ever driving M1 High to enter LoRA Sleep Mode 3!

Remediation: Open crypt_node.ioc and change PB14.Signal=GPIO_Output with label LORA_M1.
[HARDWARE GAP] Sheet /Tampering/: Anti-Tamper Circuit Unimplemented
Verification Action Item

Location: hardware/kicad/Tampering.kicad_sch (Sheet is empty; STM32 Pin 2 PC13 is marked no_connect).

Description: DESIGN_DECISIONS.md Section 1 specifies physical anti-tamper intrusion detection to wipe SE050 keys upon enclosure breach. Currently, Sheet 7 is empty and PC13 (RTC_TAMP1) is disconnected.

Remediation: Route PC13 through a microswitch with 100k pull-up and 10nF debouncing cap on Sheet Tampering.

Intentional Architecture & Design Decisions (D-01 to D-08)

Ground-Truth Authority

The following architectural choices are deliberate engineering decisions from DESIGN_DECISIONS.md. They must NOT be altered or flagged as anomalies during automated reviews.

ID Circuit Feature Engineering Justification & Hardware Rationale
D-01 Dual Isolated 3.3V LDOs
U2 (STM32) / U3 (SE050)		 Cryptographic Side-Channel Isolation: Separate LDOs prevent Differential Power Analysis (DPA) and Correlation Power Analysis (CPA) attacks. MCU switching current noise cannot modulate the SE050 power rail.
D-02 MCP73871 Safety Timer Disabled
~TE tied High to VBUS		 Load-Sharing Compatibility: System load (GPS + LoRA + MCU) draws power from MCP73871 OUT during charging. Under load, battery charges slower. Enabling the 4-6hr safety timer would cause false charge aborts.
D-03 MCP73871 SEL Pin Tied High
Pin 3 held High		 1.8A AC Adapter Mode: Allows the node to draw up to 1.8A from modern USB-C 5V supplies to support peak LoRA RF transmit bursts (~120mA+) and simultaneous 303mA battery charging.
D-04 Boost-First Power Architecture
3.7V → 5.0V Boost → 3.3V LDOs		 RF Power & PSRR: E32-900T20D requires ≥5.0V on VCC for full +20 dBm (100mW) RF output power. Regulating 5V down to 3.3V through linear LDOs provides over 65 dB PSRR, attenuating switching ripple.
D-05 WS2812B RGB LED at 3.3V
VDD on +STM_3V3		 Direct 3.3V Logic Drive: WS2812B requires $V_{IH} \ge 0.7 imes V_{DD}$. At 5V, $V_{IH} \ge 3.5 ext{V}$, causing unreliable drive from a 3.3V GPIO. Powering at 3.3V allows direct drive without level shifters.
D-06 Active P-MOSFET Protection
FDN304PZ (Q1) on Battery		 Ultra-Low Voltage Drop: Schottky diodes drop 0.3V–0.5V, wasting battery energy. FDN304PZ $R_{DS(on)} = 36 ext{ m}\Omega$, dropping less than 18 mV at 500mA load.
D-07 SE050 Autonomous I2C Wakeup
ENA, VIN, VCC on +CRYPT_3V3		 APDU Sleep Control: SE050 enters low-power sleep via T=1 APDU protocol, retaining RAM and registers, and wakes automatically upon an I2C SDA falling edge without requiring extra GPIO lines.
D-08 USB-C Universal 5V Sink
5.1kΩ pull-downs on CC1/CC2		 Universal Power Sink: Pull-downs on CC1 and CC2 signal standard 5V VBUS delivery from any USB-PD source. D+/D- are unused as firmware flashing uses the SWD header (J5).
